Our story

Décor to spice up your events.

Rahanie Events & Decor is an events and décor team based at Marsabit Plaza on Ngong Road. We plan, style and set up celebrations of every size across Nairobi and its environs — weddings and traditional ceremonies, birthdays, bridal and baby showers, graduations, corporate events, business and product launches, and intimate room, picnic and proposal set-ups.

You bring the date, the guest count and an inspo picture. We bring the drapes, florals, tableware, lighting — and the crew to put it all together, so you can walk in and enjoy it.

Not sure where to start? We also offer consultation services to help you plan your next event.

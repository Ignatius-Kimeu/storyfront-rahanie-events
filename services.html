Frequently asked

Good to know.

What info do I need to give for a quote?

Share your:

  • Budget
  • Location of the event
  • Date of event
  • Theme of the event
  • Pax (number of guests) of the event
How early should I book?

We recommend booking at least a month early to allow proper planning. The earlier the better.

Do you do events outside Nairobi?

Yes — we cover Nairobi and its environs, subject to logistics and travel arrangements.

How do I book with you?

DM us on Instagram or tap any WhatsApp button on this site and share the date, location, an inspo of what you’d love, number of guests and your preferred theme. Our team is ready to get back to you! You can also use the booking form.

How do I reach you?

Email rahaniedecors@gmail.com, call or WhatsApp 0792 353 563, or find us on Instagram at @rahanieevents.
